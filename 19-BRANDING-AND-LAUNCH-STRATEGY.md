# Identity, names, provenance and launch marketing

## Name recommendation: PULPWARD
A compact coined blend of pulp and ward/guardian that communicates defense without borrowing `Fruit Ninja` or copied brand components. **Tagline:** *One swipe from disaster.* **Secondary line:** `Guard the seed. Own the streak.` Name is a **creative working recommendation**, not registered/legal clearance. Search on 21 Nov 2026 returned non-game uses of `pulpward` (dental descriptive usage) and did not surface a clear exact-match arcade title in a quick general query; this is not exhaustive trademark, app-store, company-name or domain clearance. Have counsel/owner search WIPO, national trademark registers, gaming catalogs and desired domains before launch.

## Original character brand and logo direction
Emblem: a miniature golden Heartseed in a slant-cut coral rind with a sharp mint ribbon forming a protective semicircle, not a real-world knife. Wordmark: rounded, slightly extruded cream letters with cut-through notch in `P` and tiny sprout above `A`; dense dark plum outline retains legibility at 96px. Mascot Miri: guardianship and tactile motion; not a stock chef/ninja. App icon: Heartseed with one diagonal glowing split and two leaf shards; passes 48px silhouette test. Palette in [09](09-UI-UX-AND-VISUAL-DIRECTION.md).

## Promotional creative, anchored in actual gameplay
**15-second vertical clip:** 0–2s Heartseed and approaching dragonflare, text `Five hearts. One finger.`; 2–7s three Clean Cuts in succession; 7–10s Peel Link ricochets; 10–13s Bloom Burst; 13–15s stage result and free playable Base44 URL. Actual captured footage only, no manufactured claims about leaderboard scale or unavailable features. **Share card:** `I protected the Heartseed for 90s. Can you beat this seed?` **Launchpad copy:** `Pulpward is a fast, colorful swipe-defense arcade game. Slice corrupted fruit before it reaches your Heartseed, time perfect cuts, chain peel reactions, and challenge friends on the exact same wave. Play instantly in your browser — free, no account.`

## Launch operations
Target two tracks via the main Nitro form and Launchpad listing; ensure exact instructions at submission. Seed initial community with genuine 90s challenges and ask players what felt most satisfying. Prioritize peer-to-peer sharing of player-run Echo links over paid ads or vote incentives. Collect qualitative feedback via optional social comments, not a forced account. Do not post referral rewards tied to votes or ask for coordinated reciprocal votes.

## What not to copy
Do not reproduce commercial game UI layouts, sprites, trees, inventory text, copyrighted soundtracks or logo shapes from video, Fruit Ninja, Monopoly GO!, Subway Surfers or other references. Licensing process is in [10](10-ANIMATION-VFX-AND-AUDIO.md); contest policy in [18](18-CONTEST-COMPLIANCE-AND-SUBMISSION.md).
